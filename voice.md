# Brand voice (starter — edit me)

Who is talking: a founder who builds and ships, talking to smart operators.

Rules:
- Short sentences. Concrete nouns. Numbers over adjectives.
- Benefit first, feature second.
- No hype words: revolutionary, seamless, unleash, elevate, supercharge.
- No em dashes. Use a period, colon, or comma.
- Read it out loud. If you would not say it to a friend, rewrite it.

Example, bad: "Unleash the power of seamless hydration with our revolutionary bottle."
Example, good: "Keeps water cold for 24 hours. Weighs less than your phone."
